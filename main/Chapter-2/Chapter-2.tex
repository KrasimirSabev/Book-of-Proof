\part{Fundamentals Of Logic}

This is a quick introduction to the chapter. The idea behind this chapter is to explore logic. Logic is the discipline or the process that teaches us how to deduce information correctly. It will be an invaluable tool when we reach Theorem proving. When we are proving theorems we apply logic to the information we are considering or to information that is already known to us, via some other theorems or lemmas. If our information is correct, then so is the information we deduced (the result). \newpage
