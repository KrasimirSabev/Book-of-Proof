This Readme.md file provides a summary of the chapter notes I have written. Additionally, the list of problems after every chapter will be listed and then solved on a different page. This is in case someone wants to use these notes as a learning tool. This way one can attempt the problems before checking the answers. 

Learning methods to verify theorems. 

Chapters 1,2 lay the foundations for most of advanced mathematics. Every mathematical structure, object, or entity can be described as a set, and thus sets are fundamental. Logic allows us to understand the meanings of statements and to deduce facts about sets and to uncover further structures. 
Chapter 3 on Counting is included because it's central to a lot of topics in mathematics

Chapters 4-6 are concerned with three main techniques used for proving theorems that have the "conditional form" (P -> Q). 

Chapters 7-10 deal with *useful variations* and consequences of the proof techniques introduced in Chapters 4-6. 

Chapters 11-14 are mainly concerned with the idea of functions. Upon mastering this material, I should be ready for advanced mathematics such as Abstract Algebra, Analysis, Topology, Combinatorics, and Theory of Computation.
